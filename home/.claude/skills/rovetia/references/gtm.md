# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Current Phase

Pre-traction discovery. Product built, early testing with known contacts. Focus: finding the exact pain point that drives behavior change and willingness to pay.

## ICP (Iterating)

- **Primary**: Small veterinary clinics (1–5 people), Argentina first
- **Early adopter profile**: Younger vets (<10 years experience, recent graduates) — no entrenched workflow, more open to tech/AI tools
- **Why vets first**: Narrowed from vets + human health for focus in messaging, Apple App Store approval (human data requires additional agreements), and cleaner ICP
- **Multilingual**: App supports ES/EN; both markets viable, Argentina chosen for faster learning cycles (local context, trust-based referrals)

## Key Hypothesis (Testing)

The strongest value proposition may not be "keep patient records" (generic PMS claim) but rather:

> "Forward me the WhatsApp chaos, photos, labs, and voice notes you already receive — I'll organize it into a clean timeline."

This reduces behavior change: vets don't need to adopt a new workflow, they just forward what they already receive. The WhatsApp-forwarding feature is central to this hypothesis.

## Early Signals

- A vet (>50) used Rovetia for ~13 patients (personal relationship, not a power user)
- Referred 4 colleagues: 2 tried, 1 created ~5 patients over days then churned, 1 didn't create any
- Cold email outreach (~50 sent, hundreds collected): no responses
- Instagram presence: minimal traction
- Core learning so far: people try it, but nobody has loved it enough to stick. This is a product-positioning problem, not a headcount or awareness problem.

## GTM Approach

- **No hiring yet**: Sales/marketing hires amplify a proven process; the process isn't proven yet
- **No partner unless they bring concrete distribution, domain credibility, capital, or missing product skills**
- **Paid user interviews**: Recruit 20 young vets for 30-min discovery conversations (via vet student associations, young vet groups, Instagram communities). Budget: ~$500 total
- **Distribution partners over ads**: Vet educators, continuing education speakers, clinic modernization consultants, respected vet influencers — pay for access to 20 clinic owners (webinar, workshop, live stream), not for follower counts
- **Lead-gen VA**: One-time project to build a list of 200 young vets (Instagram, clinic emails, LinkedIn, student communities), then AI-draft personalized outreach
- **University channels**: Vet schools, recent alumni groups, residency programs — new graduates have no entrenched workflow

## 30-Day Discovery Plan

1. **Week 1**: Interview every vet who touched the product. Ask: what made you try it, what was useful, what became annoying, when did you stop, what would you miss
2. **Week 2**: Identify the single strongest use case (voice→note, WhatsApp→timeline, lab PDF→history, appointment follow-up, etc.)
3. **Week 3**: Find 20 younger vets, offer personal onboarding and first-patient migration
4. **Week 4**: Observe actual usage behavior — what gets used, what gets ignored, what drives repeat usage

## What We're Not Doing

- Instagram/Twitter ads (too early, don't know what message converts)
- Full-time marketing or sales hire
- Chasing US market before PMF in Argentina
- Broadening back to human health until vets are validated
